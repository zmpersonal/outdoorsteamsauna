Transparent scenario calculator

Turn heater kW into dollars per session.

Change every assumption yourself. No model-specific performance claim is hidden inside the result.

What the calculator assumes

Warm-up is modeled at full heater output. During the session, the estimator uses a 60% full-load-equivalent duty assumption rather than continuous maximum output.

Actual cycling, outdoor temperature, insulation, glass area, ventilation, door openings and user behavior can materially change consumption.

Formula

kWh = kW × (warm-up hours + session hours × 0.60)

cost = kWh × electricity rate
