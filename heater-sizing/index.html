Heater sizing

Size the room before you size the heater.

Calculate raw room volume, account for glass or other uninsulated surfaces, and verify the result against the exact manufacturer instructions.

Surface adjustment

This planning calculator uses 3.3 ft³ of added effective volume per ft² of uninsulated surface, the imperial form of a 1 m³-per-m² approach published in sauna-heater guidance. It is not a universal code rule.

Manufacturers can use different logic. Always use the exact heater manual or calculator for final selection.
