Heater sizing

Size the room before you size the heater.

Calculate raw room volume, then account for glass or other uninsulated surfaces before comparing the result with the current heater manufacturer’s rated room-volume range.

Surface adjustment

This planning calculator uses 3.3 ft³ of added effective volume per ft² of uninsulated surface, the imperial form of a 1 m³-per-m² approach published in sauna-heater guidance. It is not a universal building code.

Different heater manufacturers may use different sizing logic. Always use the specific heater’s current manual/calculator for final selection.
