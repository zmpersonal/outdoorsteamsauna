Outdoor sauna buyer checklist

  1. Choose the heat experience first. Traditional electric, wood-fired where permitted, infrared and hybrid systems are not substitutes.
  2. Measure usable interior space. Compare interior dimensions and bench length, not only advertised capacity.
  3. Confirm what is included. Some kits include heater and controls; others require separate equipment.
  4. Plan the site. Foundation, drainage, setbacks, exposure and freight access should be resolved before purchase.
  5. Verify electrical requirements. Use the current manual and a qualified electrician.
  6. Read delivery and assembly terms. Curbside freight, crate movement, assembly and white-glove installation are different services.
  7. Check warranty boundaries. Confirm outdoor approval, exterior treatment, roof requirements and claim coordination.
