Why this exists

An outdoor sauna in Miami and one in Minneapolis are not the same planning problem.

Outdoor traditional saunas face different warm-up loads, freeze exposure and electricity economics depending on location. This index turns public climate and energy data into standardized planning comparisons rather than pretending one national rule fits every backyard.

The score is a planning index, not a building-code or heater-approval tool. Final heater selection, clearances, wiring and installation must follow the current manufacturer instructions and local requirements.

Traditional sauna, real steam

Yes, water on hot sauna stones creates steam. It still isn't a steam room.

The domain reflects how people search. A traditional sauna uses a heater and stones; throwing appropriate water on hot stones creates a temporary humidity burst—löyly. A dedicated steam room is a different high-humidity system and requires different construction.
