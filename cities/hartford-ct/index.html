What the numbers mean for an outdoor sauna project

Hartford has a moderate climate load. Its Climate Stress Score of 50.3 is above the indexed-metro median of 37.9. The January normal low is 20.3°F, and 4 months have a normal low below freezing.

The primary planning issue in this profile is winter temperature and freeze exposure. The standardized 9 kW session estimate is $3.83, above the indexed-metro median of $2.46; this uses the Connecticut statewide residential electricity average, not a city utility quote. The selected station does not provide a complete snowfall normal, so snowfall is not reported or ranked for this city.

For construction or maintenance work that is easier in moderate conditions, the monthly normals point to April, May, June, September. This is a climate-screening observation, not a construction schedule or permit recommendation.
