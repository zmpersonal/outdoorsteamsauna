What the numbers mean for an outdoor sauna project

Cheyenne has a high climate load. Its Climate Stress Score of 55.3 is above the indexed-metro median of 37.9. The January normal low is 18.4°F, and 6 months have a normal low below freezing.

The primary planning issue in this profile is winter temperature and freeze exposure. The standardized 9 kW session estimate is $2.42, below the indexed-metro median of $2.46; this uses the Wyoming statewide residential electricity average, not a city utility quote. The selected snowfall record reports 62.9 inches annually.

For construction or maintenance work that is easier in moderate conditions, the monthly normals point to May, June, July, August. This is a climate-screening observation, not a construction schedule or permit recommendation.
