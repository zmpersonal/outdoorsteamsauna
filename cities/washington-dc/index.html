What the numbers mean for an outdoor sauna project

Washington has a moderate climate load. Its Climate Stress Score of 38.7 is above the indexed-metro median of 37.9. The January normal low is 27.8°F, and 3 months have a normal low below freezing.

The primary planning issue in this profile is electricity price. The standardized 9 kW session estimate is $3.71, above the indexed-metro median of $2.46; this uses the District of Columbia statewide residential electricity average, not a city utility quote. The selected snowfall record reports 9.7 inches annually.

For construction or maintenance work that is easier in moderate conditions, the monthly normals point to March, April, May, September. This is a climate-screening observation, not a construction schedule or permit recommendation.
