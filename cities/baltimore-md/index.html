What the numbers mean for an outdoor sauna project

Baltimore has a mild climate load. Its Climate Stress Score of 32.0 is below the indexed-metro median of 37.9. The January normal low is 30.0°F, and 2 months have a normal low below freezing.

The primary planning issue in this profile is site design and equipment selection rather than severe climate. The standardized 9 kW session estimate is $3.29, above the indexed-metro median of $2.46; this uses the Maryland statewide residential electricity average, not a city utility quote. The selected station does not provide a complete snowfall normal, so snowfall is not reported or ranked for this city.

For construction or maintenance work that is easier in moderate conditions, the monthly normals point to March, April, May, October. This is a climate-screening observation, not a construction schedule or permit recommendation.
