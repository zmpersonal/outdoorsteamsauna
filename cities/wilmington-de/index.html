What the numbers mean for an outdoor sauna project

Wilmington has a moderate climate load. Its Climate Stress Score of 43.9 is above the indexed-metro median of 37.9. The January normal low is 23.8°F, and 3 months have a normal low below freezing.

The primary planning issue in this profile is rain and exterior moisture management. The standardized 9 kW session estimate is $2.99, above the indexed-metro median of $2.46; this uses the Delaware statewide residential electricity average, not a city utility quote. The selected snowfall record reports 12.0 inches annually.

For construction or maintenance work that is easier in moderate conditions, the monthly normals point to April, May, June, September. This is a climate-screening observation, not a construction schedule or permit recommendation.
