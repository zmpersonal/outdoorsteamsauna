What the numbers mean for an outdoor sauna project

Dallas has a mild climate load. Its Climate Stress Score of 14.6 is below the indexed-metro median of 37.9. The January normal low is 37.9°F, and 0 months have a normal low below freezing.

The primary planning issue in this profile is site design and equipment selection rather than severe climate.
