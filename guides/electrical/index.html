Electrical planning

Heater kW is an energy number—not a wiring plan.

The cost calculator can estimate kWh, but breaker size, conductor size, controls, disconnects, GFCI requirements and permitted installation depend on the exact heater, control package, jurisdiction and manufacturer instructions.

Use a qualified electrician and the exact current heater manual for final electrical design.
